## Frozen outcome evaluation

New nfl_expected_opportunity_outcomes.py compares the original frozen receipt to exact recomputation from original audit and history packet, then evaluates verified final played outcomes by contest ID, GSIS ID, team, oriented matchup, position, season/date and scheduled kickoff. Require explicit final and observed times, official game/source record IDs and caller-reviewed identity. Only complete explicit integer channel components support scoring. Missing/invalid components and nonparticipation remain separate from scored zero. Summaries report same-row MAE, RMSE, signed bias and improvement counts for actual-history average, expected-history average and fixed challenger (75% receiving expected, 100% rushing expected), overall and by position.
